# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This enhancement transforms "On Product: A Field Manual" from satirical commentary into an aggressive, evidence-backed critique of product management as performative theatre. The core thesis: product management rewards political performance over production capability, and the field's most celebrated practitioners are indistinguishable from mediocre ones when measured by actual systems shipped. The enhancement targets hiring managers who can distinguish between stakeholder kabuki and production capability.

## Glossary

- **Treatise**: The HTML document "On Product: A Field Manual" (PORTFOLIO/concept2.html)
- **Theatre-PM**: Product management practiced as stakeholder performance and internal politics rather than customer delivery
- **Production-PM**: Product management measured by shipped systems that reduce operational ambiguity
- **Builder_Proof**: Concrete evidence of shipped systems with measurable outcomes (production metrics, user adoption, operational improvements)
- **Court_Credentials**: Conventional PM hiring signals (brand logos, influence theatre, stakeholder management narratives) that correlate weakly with shipping capability
- **The_Gap**: The hiring system's inability to distinguish high-performing PMs from mediocre ones based on differentiated production evidence
- **Deserter**: A PM who exits the theatre to ship actual systems, appearing disloyal to the performance
- **System**: Working software deployed to production that serves actual users and generates measurable outcomes

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Sharpen the Core Thesis with Empirical Edge

**User Story:** As a hiring manager evaluating PM candidates, I want the treatise to articulate why conventional hiring signals fail, so that I understand the structural problem with theatre-PM evaluation

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add a new section presenting the empirical observation that top-tier PM portfolios show no differentiated builder proof compared to mid-tier candidates
2. WHEN presenting the observation, THE Treatise SHALL cite the pattern of identical credential structures (strategy docs, alignment artifacts, process improvements) with zero shipped system differentiation
3. THE Treatise SHALL define "The Gap" as the hiring system's failure to screen for production capability versus performance capability
4. THE Treatise SHALL contrast Court_Credentials (logos, stakeholder narratives, roadmap artifacts) with Builder_Proof (production systems, user metrics, operational impact)
5. THE Treatise SHALL establish that if the field's winners are indistinguishable from the bulk on production metrics, the game rewards the wrong signal

### Requirement 2: Add Concrete Theatre-vs-Shipping Examples

**User Story:** As a reader skeptical of the theatre thesis, I want specific recognizable patterns of PM dysfunction, so that I can verify the critique against my own experience

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add examples illustrating the theatre-over-shipping dynamic
2. WHEN providing examples, THE Treatise SHALL include scenarios such as PRDs that never ship, alignment marathons that block execution, discovery cycles that confirm existing bias, and reorgs that reset progress
3. THE Treatise SHALL present at least 5 concrete scenarios drawn from common PM dysfunction patterns
4. THE Treatise SHALL frame each example using the historical warfare metaphors established in the maxims
5. THE Treatise SHALL position these examples as diagnostic signals that distinguish theatre-PM from production-PM

### Requirement 3: Expand the Desertion Section with Evidence-Based Positioning

**User Story:** As the author positioning myself to hiring managers, I want to demonstrate deliberate abandonment of theatre in favor of production, so that I attract opportunities aligned with building over performance

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL expand the Desertion section to position the author as someone who consciously exited the parade
2. WHEN presenting the author's positioning, THE Treatise SHALL emphasize shipping agentic systems (Moovez, Quotely) as deliberate production work outside stakeholder theatre
3. THE Treatise SHALL contrast the author's approach (systems with eval gates, operational metrics, production deployments) against conventional PM artifacts (decks, roadmaps, alignment meetings)
4. THE Treatise SHALL frame the author's GitHub portfolio, eval frameworks, and deployed systems as builder proof that does not speak court language
5. THE Treatise SHALL acknowledge this positioning deliberately filters out theatre-PM opportunities while attracting production-PM roles

### Requirement 4: Add "The Emperor Has No Clothes" Section

**User Story:** As a hiring manager frustrated by PM hiring, I want evidence that conventional evaluation criteria fail to identify top performers, so that I understand why to consider non-traditional signals

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add a new section titled "The Emperor Has No Clothes" examining the undifferentiated nature of PM portfolios
2. WHEN analyzing portfolios, THE Treatise SHALL observe that senior PM portfolios universally contain the same artifacts (strategy frameworks, influence stories, process improvements) regardless of actual production output
3. THE Treatise SHALL note the absence of shipped system differentiation at the top of the field
4. THE Treatise SHALL argue that if winners and losers have identical portfolio structures, the evaluation system is broken
5. THE Treatise SHALL present this as evidence that the field optimizes for performance rather than production

### Requirement 5: Strengthen Historical Warfare Metaphors with Modern PM Patterns

**User Story:** As a reader entertained by the satirical tone, I want additional maxims connecting historical warfare to modern PM dysfunction, so that the critique remains sharp and memorable

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add 6 to 10 new maxims following the established EARS pattern: WHEN [historical event], THE [PM pattern] SHALL [theatre outcome]
2. WHEN adding maxims, THE Treatise SHALL draw from additional historical sources (Roman Empire politics, French court intrigue, Byzantine bureaucracy, Cold War proxy conflicts)
3. THE Treatise SHALL focus maxims on observable PM dysfunction patterns (vanity metrics, priority theatre, reorg churn, transformation initiatives that preserve the status quo)
4. THE Treatise SHALL maintain the satirical tone and parallel structure established in existing maxims
5. THE Treatise SHALL ensure each maxim illuminates a specific theatre-PM antipattern

### Requirement 6: Add Diagnostic Questions for Hiring Managers

**User Story:** As a hiring manager reading this treatise, I want practical questions to distinguish theatre-PM from production-PM candidates, so that I can improve my evaluation process

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add a new section providing diagnostic interview questions
2. WHEN providing questions, THE Treatise SHALL focus on eliciting builder proof (systems shipped, metrics moved, operational improvements) rather than court credentials (alignment stories, stakeholder management, process adoption)
3. THE Treatise SHALL include at least 8 questions that expose whether a candidate ships systems or performs alignment
4. THE Treatise SHALL frame questions to distinguish production capability from performance capability
5. THE Treatise SHALL provide contrast examples showing how theatre-PM versus production-PM candidates answer each question differently

### Requirement 7: Add Evidence-Based "Why the Game Rewards Theatre" Section

**User Story:** As a reader who ships systems but struggles to get hired, I want to understand the structural incentives that reward theatre over production, so that I recognize the game is not broken—it is working as designed for the wrong objectives

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add a section analyzing why organizations structurally reward theatre-PM over production-PM
2. WHEN analyzing incentives, THE Treatise SHALL examine factors such as legibility of influence artifacts versus systems work, internal visibility of alignment theatre versus external customer impact, and career progression through scope defense versus shipped outcomes
3. THE Treatise SHALL note that theatre-PM generates visible internal artifacts consumed by promotion committees while production-PM generates external outcomes invisible to internal stakeholders
4. THE Treatise SHALL argue that organizations optimize hiring for cultural fit to existing theatre dynamics rather than production capability
5. THE Treatise SHALL position this as an equilibrium outcome, not a fixable bug—the game is working correctly for internal political objectives

### Requirement 8: Enhance Visual Design to Support Aggressive Tone

**User Story:** As a reader encountering this treatise, I want the visual presentation to reinforce the satirical critique, so that form matches function

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL maintain the existing historical manuscript aesthetic (gilt rules, Cormorant Garamond, parchment colors)
2. WHEN adding new sections, THE Treatise SHALL use visual hierarchy to emphasize key empirical claims (The Gap, The Emperor Has No Clothes, structural incentive analysis)
3. THE Treatise SHALL add visual callouts or styling for diagnostic questions and contrast examples
4. THE Treatise SHALL ensure new content integrates seamlessly with existing maxims and glossary styling
5. THE Treatise SHALL preserve the crimson "Desertion" chapter styling as the climactic position

### Requirement 9: Add Closing Call-to-Action for Production-Oriented Hiring Managers

**User Story:** As a hiring manager convinced by the critique, I want a clear path to contact the author, so that I can evaluate them for production-PM roles

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL add a closing section explicitly addressing hiring managers who distinguish production from performance
2. WHEN addressing hiring managers, THE Treatise SHALL acknowledge that this treatise deliberately repels theatre-PM opportunities
3. THE Treatise SHALL position the author's portfolio (GitHub systems, eval frameworks, deployed agents) as builder proof for production-PM evaluation
4. THE Treatise SHALL provide direct contact paths (email reveal, LinkedIn, portfolio links)
5. THE Treatise SHALL frame the call-to-action as filtering for alignment: if you hire for theatre, close the tab; if you hire for production, here is the proof

### Requirement 10: Maintain Satirical Tone While Adding Substance

**User Story:** As a reader, I want the enhanced treatise to remain entertaining and quotable while becoming more substantive, so that it functions as both critique and positioning document

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Treatise SHALL preserve the established satirical voice (Clausewitzian framing, historical warfare metaphors, court terminology)
2. WHEN adding empirical observations, THE Treatise SHALL integrate evidence within the satirical framework rather than shifting to academic or neutral tone
3. THE Treatise SHALL ensure new maxims, examples, and sections maintain the entertainment value of existing content
4. THE Treatise SHALL balance aggressive critique with memorability and shareability
5. THE Treatise SHALL avoid defensiveness—the tone must remain confident that shipping systems is the rational strategy even if the hiring market does not reward it
